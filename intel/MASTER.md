## FIELD TRIP (Oct 8-9)
**Focus**: Catchment dynamics → coastal evolution continuum

**Themes**:
- Land degradation & desertification drivers
- Forest fire geomorphological footprint
- Runoff erosion processes
- Agricultural terraces & dams impact on sediment budgets
- Hydro-geomorphological hazards (flash floods, Peritsis river)
- Coastal pressures

**Stops**: Alyko, Laguna, Pyrgaki
**Topics at coast**: Cliff retreat, dune erosion, Nature-based Solutions (NbS) for climate adaptation

## WORKSHOP AIMS
Bring geoscientists together to advance understanding of:
- Denudation processes (weathering, erosion, mass movements)
- Coastal and terrestrial environments
- Drivers, rates, diversity, variability worldwide
- Facilitate future collaborations
