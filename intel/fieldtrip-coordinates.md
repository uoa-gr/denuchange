## Notes

- **Aplomata**: VERIFIED - Near Grotta district in Naxos Chora. Wikipedia: "The name comes from the Venetian word Grota, meaning 'cave', referring to the many small caves under the cliff at Aplomata."
- **Faneromeni Dam**: VERIFIED - 37m earthfill dam built in 1992 (Hydrogroup study). AllTrails POI ID 1930513 confirms location. CNN article mentions it as one of 3 dams on Naxos.
- **Laguna**: "Laguna Beach Park" on OSM - palaeogeographical site (ancient harbor for Dionysus temple at Yria)
- **Stelida**: Hamlet with archaeological significance, tafoni formations

## Content from ODT (for descriptions)

### Day 1 Stops

**Aplomata**: Landslide hazard area

**Faneromeni Dam**: Water management infrastructure

**Kinidaros**: Area affected by forest fires. Discussion of fire impact on landscape, erosion processes.

**Apeiranthos**: Small mountainous village in center of Naxos. Geological Museum visit - introduction to geological, geomorphological, and palaeogeographical history. Virtual emery mine visit.
- URL: https://www.naxos.gr/geological-museum-of-apeiranthos/?lang=en

### Day 2 Stops

**Laguna**:
- Local geomorphology and palaeogeographical evolution
- During Antiquity, was a bay used as harbor for nearby Dionysus temple at Yria
- Granodiorite of Naxos - formation and composition
- Spectacular honeycomb weathering (tafoni) on crystalline rocks
- Aeolianites (partly quarried in ancient times)
- Beachrocks - formation and coastal protection role
- Nature-based solutions vs hard engineering

**Stelida**: Tafoni, weathering processes

**Mikri Vigla**:
- Protection of coastal vulnerable environments
- Climate change and sea level rise

**Pyrgaki**:
- Majestic sand dunes (>5m tall) around the beach

**Alyko**:
- Sandy pocket beach at front of steep coastal cliff
- Coastal erosion processes and assessment methods
- GNSS-RTK for topographic cross sections and coastline change measurement
